# Feytopia 2026 - Season 7 - Events Schedule
This document summarizes unique events, workshops, presentations, talks, and activities announced in the Feytopia 2026 Season 7 Signal group from December 1, 2025, to March 18, 2026. Daily meals and specific party announcements are excluded.
---
## Recurring Activities & Workshops Summary
*   **Community Meetings:** (Weekly, various leads). A regular gathering for alignment, sharing, and shaping community direction. Periodicity: Weekly, typically Friday/Monday evening.
*   **Disco Dishwashing:** (Daily after dinner, various volunteers). A communal activity involving washing dishes, often accompanied by music. Periodicity: Daily.
    *   *Note on process:* Participants are reminded to pre-wash dishes before placing them in the sanitizer, and to clean common dishwashing areas. Newcomers receive onboarding on the "dish flow."
*   **Sauna Sessions:** (Daily, various volunteers for heating/maintenance). The sauna is regularly heated for community use, with updates on temperature and usage guidelines (towels, water, logging, showering before hot tub use). Periodicity: Daily, often multiple times a day.
    *   *Note on process:* Users are reminded not to sit directly on wood, to add logs when leaving, and to bring water. A website (`jemstopes.co/isithot`) provides live temperature updates.
*   **GGGG (Greek Gods and Goddesses Gym) / Workouts:** (Regularly, various leads). Fitness sessions, often with specific themes or focuses (e.g., HIIT, bodyweight, partner exercises, human pyramids, jujitsu). Periodicity: Daily or several times a week.
*   **YYYY (Your Yonne iYengar Yoga) / Yoga Sessions:** (Regularly, various leads). Yoga classes with different focuses (e.g., spinal decompression, hip flexors, crow poses, slow-flow, dynamic vinyasa). Periodicity: Several times a week.
*   **Morning Zen Club / Meditation & Walks:** (Daily, Didi). A morning ritual including silent forest baths, meditation, and journaling. Periodicity: Daily, typically 7:00-8:30 AM.
*   **Meditation Club:** (Regularly, various leads). Sessions for communal meditation, sometimes guided, sometimes silent. Periodicity: Several times a week.
*   **Sharing Circles / Emotional Ecology:** (Weekly, various facilitators). Safe, closed containers for sharing emotions, thoughts, and reflections, often focusing on cultivating deeper presence and connection. Periodicity: Weekly, typically Thursday/Friday evening.
*   **Lightning Talks / Impromptu Divagations:** (Weekly, various presenters). Short (5-8 minute) presentations on passions, curiosities, projects, or any topic. A "no corpo speak" rule is often encouraged. Periodicity: Weekly, typically Thursday/Friday evening.
*   **Honesty Bar Management:** (Ongoing, Lea/Poli/Jessica Flore Angel). A community-managed bar. Regular reminders are sent for payment (via QR code) and requests for donations to cover deficits. Users are asked to be mindful of consumption and not to borrow ingredients from the main kitchen for personal use. Periodicity: Regular updates.
*   **Lost & Found:** (Ongoing, various individuals). Items found in common areas are collected and announced. A "Lost & Found" cabinet is located in the main salon.
*   **Chateau Cleanups / Sacred Sexy Sunday Scrub (SSSS):** (Weekly, various leads). A collective, themed cleaning ritual for common areas, often accompanied by music and focused tasks. Periodicity: Weekly, typically Sunday afternoon.
    *   *Note on process:* Emphasis on tidying, gathering lost items, and cleaning floors.
